EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (10 lines)
================================================================
1. The /rittenhouse-square 6-page hub (guide, history, public art, things to do, restaurants, where to stay) SHIPS TODAY and is excluded from this report; six items below are deliberately scoped as spokes that hub can link down into, not overlaps.
2. The site's real asset is asymmetric: 63 primary documents, of which only 7 are referenced by any page — every history build below is an interlink job as much as a writing job.
3. Two lenses produced genuine, checkable scoops nobody else on the web has: the Congressional Union's Pennsylvania headquarters at 1820 Pine (the house next door, 1914-15), and the proof that 1822 Pine was 756 Pine before the 1857 renumbering.
4. The commercial side is the opposite picture: high-intent group-travel queries (parking, feeding 16, reunion lodging, accessibility) are answered publicly only by 5-10 year old forum threads and marketplaces — cheap wins, fastest revenue.
5. Grade-A verifiability (sources in hand, free, linkable) covers roughly two-thirds of the list; the archive-dependent items are ranked lower on purpose.
6. Highest link-earning potential sits in three places: the renumbering/dating methodology, the 1820 Pine adjacency, and the Roset-sisters thread that runs from this house to Drexel University and to Jane Addams's Hull-House.
7. Several existing claims must be corrected inside these builds, not in a separate cleanup pass: "Victorian mansion" (the City says late Greek Revival), "$25,000 renovation" ($6,000, 1899), "Betsy Ross House architects" (that was Okie alone, post-1918), and the Drexel framing.
8. Two structural blockers should be fixed before publishing volume: the 1822pine.com / rittenhouseresidence.com duplicate-domain canonical question, and the duplicated "-2" archive entries.
9. Everything wedding-adjacent is written as LODGING and photography logistics only — site policy is no parties/events, and the "Events & Filming" story chapter contradicts it and needs reconciling.
10. Recommended first sprint: items 1-5. Recommended first quarter: items 1-12.

--- 4. THE SUFFRAGE HEADQUARTERS NEXT DOOR: 1820 PINE STREET, 1914-15

Target queries: congressional union woman suffrage philadelphia headquarters / 1820 pine street philadelphia / alice paul philadelphia / national woman's party pennsylvania headquarters

Verifiability: A — three Internet Archive issue URLs in hand. Hard guardrail to publish inside the piece: only 3 of 161 issues name the address; no document links the two houses; say so.

--- 5. BEFORE IT WAS 1822: HOW WE DATED THIS HOUSE, AND THE YEAR PHILADELPHIA RENUMBERED EVERY DOOR
Angle: The flagship methodology piece. 756 Pine became 1822 Pine under the 1856 ordinance (adopted in McElroy's with the 1858 volume), proven with an eight-household side-by-side conversion table at a constant +1066 offset. Then the dating: zero occupants anywhere at 740-769 Pine in the 1845, 1850, 1852 and 1853 directories; the row appears in 1854 and is full in 1855. Refutes Redfin's "circa 1845" and OPA's estimated "1800", and independently corroborates the April 1854 McCrea-to-Roset deed.
Target queries: 756 pine street philadelphia / philadelphia house renumbering 1857 / schuylkill front street philadelphia old names / how to find out when my house was built philadelphia / 1822 pine street history
Why it can win: It owns a zero-competition long-tail cluster AND supplies a worked example for a wall every Philadelphia genealogist and house-historian hits. It also converts the site's weakest published claim (the 1854 build date) into a demonstrated one.
Verifiability: A on the directory evidence (all volumes free and full-text on Internet Archive); publish the honest caveats — canvassing lag, absence ≠ non-existence, and that quotes are from raw OCR and should be confirmed against page images. B on the 1995 nomination's even-side row date, which did not survive OCR and needs one person to read PDF p.88.
Effort: M (the research is done; the conversion table and figure captions take time).
Link-earning: HIGHEST on this list. Methodology + free-source pages are what local-history and genealogy sites link to.
Builds on: /history/provenance, /history/timeline, the 1854 deed abstract document page, the whole archive.
